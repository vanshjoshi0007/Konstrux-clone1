🔵 Prepared for Builders & Construction Companies

KONSTRUX replaces WhatsApp threads, Excel sheets and paper registers with one connected system. What the site engineer logs on the phone is, the same second, the report the office sees.

Konstrux is a construction management platform built around two connected experiences: a mobile app for the Site Engineer working on the ground, and an Admin Panel for the Owner or company managing the business across every site. Both draw from the same live data, so nothing needs to be re-entered, re-typed, or reconciled by hand between the site and the office. The sections below walk through every feature category and module, with a screenshot of the actual screen alongside a description of what it does and why it matters.

WhatsApp, Excel and paper registers — three tools that never talk to each other.

By the time updates reach the office, they're hours late, unverified, and impossible to compare across sites.

Delayed visibility

Updates arrive hours or days late, buried inside chat threads no one has time to re-read.

No proof, no accountability

Cash expenses go unrecorded with no photo, no bill, and no audit trail to check against.

Disputed payments

Labour dues tracked verbally or on a torn register page — a familiar source of arguments.

Budget surprises

Overruns get discovered at month-end reconciliation, not the day they actually happen.

Owner dependency

Nothing moves without a phone call to every single site manager, every single day.

KONSTRUX closes the gap

One entry at the source feeds every report — nothing re-typed, nothing reconciled by hand.

One home screen for daily site operations.

Eight modules cover the full working day — from the morning attendance mark to the evening ledger check.

Daily Progress Report

Track daily site progress with measurements, work status, contractor updates, photos, and complete project history in one place.

Expense Tracking

Record material, transport, diesel, labour, rent, and miscellaneous expenses with bills, receipts, and payment details.

Labour Attendance

Manage contractor, staff, and temporary labour attendance with daily presence, cost summaries, and payroll-ready records.

Material Requests

Create, approve, and track material requests from construction sites with complete quantity and delivery history.

Live Inventory

Generate purchase orders, monitor received materials, compare requested vs delivered quantities, and reduce procurement errors

Machinery Usage

Track machine allocation, operating hours, maintenance schedules, fuel usage, and equipment availability.

Labour Payments

Manage wages, contractor payments, pending balances, payment history, and complete financial records.

Site Ledger Book

Maintain detailed contractor accounts including advances, expenses, deductions, payments, and outstanding balances.
